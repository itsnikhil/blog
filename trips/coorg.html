12:00 — 14:00

Mandalpatti 🌄

Weather dependentBig viewsShared jeep~₹500/person

Private cars stop at a checkpoint — from there it's a bumpy 30-minute shared-jeep ride and a short 5-minute hike to the top, roughly 4,000 ft up. If the weather and roads are kind, we go. If not, lunch + coffee wins.

No pressure. The trip stays good either way.
If we have extra time: Dubare Elephant Camp is open 9–11 AM and 4:30–5:30 PM (₹50 entry, cash only) for elephant bathing and feeding — an easy add-on, or a good backup if Mandalpatti gets rained out.

GOOD TO KNOW
A few practical
things.
🪪

Carry ID

A government ID is needed at rental pickup — keep it on hand through the trip, not just for the handover.

🌦️

Best time to visit

Early Sept is just past peak monsoon — green, misty, a little unpredictable. Post-monsoon and winter are Coorg's calmest months overall.

⏰

Start early

Most sights and viewpoints close by 6 PM — mornings stretch the day further than evenings do.

💵

Cash on hand

Raja's Seat, Nisargadhama, and the Mandalpatti jeep stand are cheap but usually cash-only.

🌿

Leave it better

Skip single-use plastic where we can — small habit, real difference in the hills.

🚗

The road itself

NH275 via Mysuru → Kushalnagar is the reliable route in — no ghat sections to worry about on this side.
